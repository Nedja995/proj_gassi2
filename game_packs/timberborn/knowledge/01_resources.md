# Timberborn Resource Mechanics (v0.6)

## Logs

Logs come from Lumberjack Flags. Each flag designates a cutting area. Beavers
cut mature trees within the flag radius. Log output depends on tree density and
how many beavers are assigned to the flag.

Birch trees are the primary early-game log source. Pine trees grow faster but
yield fewer logs per tree. Mixed forests give the best sustained output.

One lumberjack flag typically supports 1–3 beavers efficiently. Assigning more
beavers than trees can support causes idle time without improving output.

## Planks

Planks (Daske) are always the first bottleneck. They require a Sawmill plus
Lumber Yard — both must be built and staffed.

Sawmill production: approximately 1 log → 1 plank at base efficiency. Each
sawmill requires 1 beaver worker. Lumber Yards store finished planks.

Early game: build the Sawmill as early as possible — almost every building
requires planks. Prioritise plank stockpile before expanding population.

A useful rule: if planks are below 20 and population is above 10, stop all
non-essential construction and let planks recover before queuing new buildings.

## Food

Food sources by tier:

Berries (Blueberry Bush): zero processing, lowest calorie density, no building
needed beyond forager hut. Best early-game food source.

Carrots and Potatoes: require farmhouse, irrigation, and harvester. Mid-game
staples with higher density than berries.

Bread: requires wheat farm → windmill (for grinding) → bakery. Highest calorie
density. Best for large populations in mid-to-late game.

Food consumption: each beaver consumes approximately 600 calories per day.
Monitor the food bar in the HUD — a declining trend means supply is below demand.

## Water

Clean water is required for survival and for farms. Beavers drink from wells or
direct water tiles. If a well runs dry during drought a colony can collapse quickly.

Water Tank capacity: one Water Tank stores approximately 3,500 units of water.
Building multiple tanks before each drought is the main survival strategy.

During a wet season the river refills — use this window to build additional dams
and tanks, not just to refill existing stores.

## Science

Science is produced by the Observatory building. Each Observatory generates
science passively while staffed. Science unlocks technology tiers.

Priority tech unlocks in order: Irrigation (for farms), Badwater handling,
Advanced Power (mechanical batteries), District Crossing.

## Metal

Metal is a late-game resource. It requires a Metal Deposit, a Metal Extractor
building, and a Metal Workshop for processing into metal blocks or parts.

Metal is needed for advanced machines: Metal Mechanist Hut, advanced power
storage, and high-tier tools. Do not over-invest in metal before population
is stable above 40 beavers.
