# Timberborn Districts, Expansion and Labour (v0.6)

## District System

A District is a self-contained settlement area. All buildings in a district
share a single labour pool — beavers work any building within their district.

Beavers cannot cross between districts unless a District Crossing connects
them. District Crossings are expensive (30 logs, 50 planks) so plan the
first crossing carefully.

When to add a second district: when the primary district is fully developed,
resource sources in the primary area are depleted, or a second water source
on another part of the map needs exploitation.

## Labour Pool Management

Each building has a worker slot (or multiple). Unassigned buildings sit idle.
Assign workers strategically — production bottleneck buildings (Sawmill,
Water Pump, Forester) should always be fully staffed.

Idle beavers (shown in population panel) mean you have spare labour. Use
idle beavers to staff secondary production or construction.

If total population is below 15 do not spread workers thin. Prioritise:
1. Forester (replanting)
2. Sawmill (planks)
3. Water Pump (water supply)
4. Farmhouse (food)
5. Everything else

## Builder Beavers

Construction is handled by beavers with the Builder role. Too few builders
slows all construction; too many builders reduces production workers.

A ratio of approximately 20–25% of total population as builders is efficient
in early-to-mid game. Reduce builder count once the main construction wave
is complete and shift them to production roles.

## Worker Priority and Shift Scheduling

Buildings can be set to priority mode (yellow priority icon). Priority
buildings are staffed first when workers are scarce.

Always set priority on: Water Pump, Sawmill, Forester.
Deprioritise: entertainment buildings, decorative structures.

Shift scheduling allows splitting a building's work between day and night
shifts. Use this on the Sawmill and Water Pump if you have odd worker
counts — a 0.5 shift assignment means a beaver works half-time there and
half-time elsewhere, reducing waste.

## Expansion Timing

Expand population only when food and water are stable for at least
2 drought cycles ahead. Rapid population expansion without sufficient
food infrastructure causes colony-wide starvation.

Safe expansion signal: food bar stable or growing, water reserve above
2x next drought duration estimate, planks above 30.

Dangerous signal: planks below 10, food bar declining, drought imminent
(less than 5 wet-season days remaining on the drought timer).
