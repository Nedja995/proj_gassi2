# Nebuchadnezzar Resource Mechanics (v1.0)

## Food Resources

Food is the foundation of every city. Multiple food types are required to
evolve housing beyond basic shacks. Each additional food type unlocks a
higher housing tier.

Grain: the primary staple. Grown on grain farms, stored in granaries.
Grain is seasonal — it grows only in specific months. Plan harvests so
granaries are full before the off-season.

Dates: second food type. Date palm farms produce year-round with no
seasonal restriction. Dates are the easiest second food source to establish.

Fish: third food type. Requires proximity to the river and a fishing wharf.
High yield but location-constrained — only tiles adjacent to water support
fishing wharfs.

Meat: fourth food type. Requires a cattle farm and a slaughterhouse.
More complex supply chain than fish but not river-dependent.

Bread: highest-tier food. Requires grain → bakery. Bread counts as a
separate food type from raw grain. Required for noble estate evolution.

Beer: produced from grain at a brewery. Counts as a goods/luxury item,
not a food — but is required for some housing evolution stages.

## Building Materials

Clay: extracted from clay pits. Used to make bricks (fired in kilns).
Clay bricks are the primary construction material for all mid-to-late
game buildings.

Reeds: harvested from reed beds near the river. Used for construction
of basic buildings and for making baskets/rope.

Bitumen: extracted from bitumen seeps. Used for waterproofing and
as a construction sealant for advanced buildings.

Wood: imported via trade or obtained from the map if timber is available.
Required for planks, furniture, and certain production buildings.

Bricks: clay fired in a kiln. Each kiln requires clay input and a worker.
Brick production rate determines how fast you can build. Always run kilns
at full capacity when constructing.

## Luxury Goods and Trade Goods

Pottery: clay shaped and fired. Required for housing evolution and bazaar
distribution to mid-tier houses.

Linen cloth: woven from flax. Required for higher housing tiers.

Jewellery: produced from gold. Required for noble estates.

Oil: pressed from sesame. Required for advanced housing and temples.

Beer: fermented from grain. Required for entertainment buildings and housing.

Wine: produced from grapes if available on the map. High-prestige good.

## Workers and Labor

Workers are produced by housing. Each housing tier produces more workers
per occupied tile. Shacks produce the fewest workers; noble estates produce
the most.

Unemployment rate is shown in the status bar. Unemployment above 10%
means surplus labour — safe to add more production buildings.
Unemployment near 0% means you need more evolved housing before expanding
industry.

Bronze tools: required for higher-tier production buildings (advanced
quarries, jewellery workshops, certain craft buildings). Tools are produced
at a bronze workshop from copper and tin. Establish tool production before
attempting late-game buildings.
