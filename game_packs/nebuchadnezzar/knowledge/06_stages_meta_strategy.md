# Nebuchadnezzar Game Stages and Meta-Strategy (v1.0)

## Three Game Stages

### Early Game (Missions 1-3, population < 500)

Goal: establish a functioning food and distribution loop before expanding.

Priority order:
1. Road grid — build roads first, before housing
2. Grain farm + granary — food supply
3. Kiln + clay pit — brick production for construction
4. Bazaar centrally placed in the housing zone
5. Date farm — second food type to unlock tier 2 housing
6. Well — water access for tier 3 housing
7. Pottery workshop — goods for tier 3 housing
8. Entertainment building — prevent early approval drops

Common early mistake: building too much housing before establishing supply.
More shacks than your food supply can serve causes immediate approval drops.
Build supply infrastructure first, then housing.

### Mid Game (Missions 4-7, population 500-2000)

Goal: evolve housing to large villas, manage multi-commodity supply chains.

Key tasks:
- Second bazaar when walker coverage gaps appear
- Second food type fully operational (fish or meat for tier 3-4)
- Linen production online for tier 4 housing
- Beer production for entertainment and housing
- Second granary near production hub
- Entertainment buildings with walker coverage across all housing
- Trade post if mission requires money objective

Bottleneck check: if housing evolution has stalled, check the walker
coverage map and identify which requirement is missing. Usually it is
goods at the bazaar or walker range gaps.

### Late Game (Missions 7+, population 2000+)

Goal: prestige buildings, noble estate evolution, monument construction.

Key tasks:
- Large temple for prestige + religion coverage
- Fourth food type (bread or meat) for noble estate evolution
- Jewellery production (gold workshop) for tier 5 goods requirement
- Monument construction — requires large brick and material stockpile
- Secondary bazaar network for expanded city
- Bronze tool production for advanced craft buildings
- Trade surplus in high-value goods (linen, oil, jewellery)

## Common Failure Modes

Approval collapse: food supply interrupted (seasonal gap, granary empty,
bazaar not stocked). Fix: check granary stock, check bazaar panel,
trace supply chain from farm to bazaar.

Housing evolution stall: one requirement missing across the whole district.
Fix: open a house tile panel and read which requirements are met/missing.
Address the single missing requirement.

Labor shortage: too many production buildings, not enough evolved housing.
Fix: pause building new production, focus on evolving existing housing to
increase workers per tile.

Brick shortage: construction queue paused because kilns are not keeping up.
Fix: add another clay pit and kiln pair. Run kilns continuously.

Money objective missed: insufficient trade surplus. Fix: ensure goods
production exceeds city consumption, open trade routes at trade post,
check that trade post has road access to city road network.

## Useful Ratios (v1.0 baseline)

- Housing tiles per bazaar: 20-40 (depending on road layout)
- Wells per 25 housing tiles: 1
- Grain farms per bakery: 2-3 (seasonal gap coverage)
- Date farms per 30 houses: 1-2 (year-round, high yield)
- Kilns per active construction phase: 2-4
- Entertainment buildings per 40 housing tiles: 1
- Temples per housing district: 1 small temple per 50 tiles,
  1 large temple per district for prestige missions
